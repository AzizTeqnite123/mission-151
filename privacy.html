Privacy

LAST UPDATED · APRIL 2026

This site is operated by Victor Marx for Governor. We collect a minimum of personal information for one purpose: running Mission 151 and the campaigns that follow it.

What we collect

  • Registration: first name, last name, email, county, and (optionally) mobile number.
  • Check-in: email and county.
  • Captain applications: the same registration data plus your stated interest in leading your county.
  • Server-side technical data: IP address and request headers, retained briefly for fraud and abuse prevention.

Why we collect it

To confirm registrations, deliver event reminders, drive the live county map on May 6, 2026, and contact registrants about future operations. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

SMS messages

We only send text messages to people who provide a mobile number and check the SMS consent box. You can reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

Cookies

We use a small number of first-party cookies to keep your check-in session in sync with the live broadcast. We do not use third-party advertising trackers on this site.

Your choices

To unsubscribe from email, use the link in any message we send you. To request deletion of your data, email privacy@mission151.com.
